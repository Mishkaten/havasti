# Havasti · Հավաստի

**Name:** Havasti (Հավաստի) means "reliable, certified" in Armenian. Short, easy to say in hy/ru/en/de.
**Tagline:** hy "Վավերացված թարգմանություն՝ առանց հերթերի" · en "Certified translation, without the queues" · ru "Заверенный перевод без очередей"

## Logo
`assets/mark.svg`: navy seal, dashed apricot ring, a document with a folded corner, a pomegranate check badge. Reads as "stamped and approved". `assets/favicon.svg` is the simplified version for small sizes.

## Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink (navy) | #1B2340 | text, seal, dark bands |
| Apricot | #F28C28 | primary buttons, accents (Armenia's apricot) |
| Pomegranate | #A4243B | prices, check marks, eyebrows (Armenia's pomegranate) |
| Paper | #FBF6EC | page background |
| Stone | #E9E1D0 | borders |

The red / navy / apricot stripe at the top of the page nods to the flag without copying it.

## Type
Headings: Noto Serif Armenian (Source Serif 4 for Cyrillic). Body: Inter with Noto Sans Armenian fallback.

## Voice
Formal "you" (Դուք / Вы / you), short sentences, always state price, date and next step. No em dashes.

## Edit before launch
Contact details, prices and fees are placeholders at the top of `app.js` (`CONFIG`).
